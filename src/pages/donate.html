Support Our Mission

Your support keeps trained marine professionals, vessels, and equipment ready for the agencies and communities of the Delta. Azul Marine Group is a California 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and contributions are tax deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Ways to give

Financial support goes toward equipment and vessel readiness for agency-requested assistance, training in boating safety and rescue operations, and stewardship work on the Sacramento, San Joaquin, and Mokelumne Rivers.
