About Azul Marine Group

A California nonprofit public benefit corporation, based in Discovery Bay, that brings marine professionals together to support maritime emergencies, deliver marine training, and care for the waterways of the Delta.

Who We Are

A community of marine professionals

Azul Marine Group (AMG) is made up of people who work on and around the water: search and rescue personnel, divers, salvage specialists, marine firefighters, marine engineers, and licensed captains. The organization exists so that this experience can be offered, in an organized way, to the public agencies responsible for emergencies on the Delta and to the communities that live along it.

The organization is incorporated in California as a nonprofit public benefit corporation and is recognized by the Internal Revenue Service as a 501(c)(3) public charity. Its public presence began with an introductory announcement on Facebook in March 2025.

Our Mission

The Azul Marine Group brings together a community of marine professionals from various disciplines, including Search and Rescue, Diving, Salvage, Marine Firefighting, Marine Engineering, and Licensed Captains, to support maritime emergencies and promote marine safety and education.

We are dedicated to collaborating with local, regional, state, and federal agencies, providing assistance during maritime emergencies and delivering specialized training in areas such as boating safety, rescue operations, marine engineering, and environmental conservation.

Our goal is to protect and enhance the Sacramento, San Joaquin, and Mokelumne Rivers' ecosystems while promoting sustainability through research, water quality testing, and levee integrity inspections. We strive to support our communities through expert advice, vessel support, and training programs that safeguard lives, property, and the environment.

How we work with agencies

AMG does not replace any public agency and does not self-dispatch. Assistance is provided at the request of, and in coordination with, the agency that has responsibility for an incident. Between incidents, the organization's focus is training, education, and stewardship work that makes the next response better.

  • Assistance on request. Local, regional, state, and federal agencies can request support during maritime emergencies.
  • Training and exercises. Members train in boating safety, rescue operations, marine engineering, and environmental conservation, and take part in joint exercises with partners.
  • Stewardship. Research, water quality testing, and levee integrity inspections on the Sacramento, San Joaquin, and Mokelumne Rivers.

Who we serve

  • Public agencies at the local, regional, state, and federal level.
  • Emergency responders working maritime incidents who need specialized marine disciplines alongside them.
  • Delta communities, including residents, boaters, and businesses, through boating safety education and a safer waterway.
  • River ecosystems of the Sacramento, San Joaquin, and Mokelumne Rivers.
