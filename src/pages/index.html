Training in Action

Open Water Large Vessel Training

Captains and rescue swimmers of Pacific Coast Water Rescue trained alongside five agencies in a State Fire Training exercise, Open Water Large Vessel, hosted by the Woodbridge Fire Department.

Interagency training like this is at the center of Azul Marine Group's mission: building the skills, coordination, and trust that make the next response on the water safer.

Where We Serve

Sacramento, the San Joaquin Delta, and the Bay Area

Azul Marine Group supports agencies and communities across more than a thousand miles of connected waterways, from the Sacramento River through the Delta to San Francisco Bay.

  • Sacramento River corridor
  • San Joaquin River and the Delta
  • Mokelumne River
  • San Francisco Bay and Bay Area waterways

Who We Are

A community of marine professionals

Azul Marine Group brings together search and rescue personnel, divers, salvage specialists, marine firefighters, marine engineers, and licensed captains. We assist local, regional, state, and federal agencies during maritime emergencies, deliver marine training, and care for the Sacramento, San Joaquin, and Mokelumne Rivers.
