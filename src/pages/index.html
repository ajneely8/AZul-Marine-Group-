Our Mission

Assistance when agencies need it. Training and stewardship the rest of the year.

The Azul Marine Group brings together a community of marine professionals from various disciplines to support maritime emergencies and promote marine safety and education.

We are dedicated to collaborating with local, regional, state, and federal agencies, providing assistance during maritime emergencies and delivering specialized training in areas such as boating safety, rescue operations, marine engineering, and environmental conservation.

Our goal is to protect and enhance the ecosystems of the Sacramento, San Joaquin, and Mokelumne Rivers while promoting sustainability through research, water quality testing, and levee integrity inspections. We support our communities through expert advice, vessel support, and training programs that safeguard lives, property, and the environment.

Who We Serve

Agencies, communities, and the waterways of the Delta

Our work is organized around the people and places named in our mission. We do not replace public agencies. We support them.

Public agencies

Local, regional, state, and federal agencies that request assistance during maritime emergencies, or that need vessel support and expert advice.

Emergency responders

Responders working a maritime incident who need trained divers, boat crews, salvage know-how, or marine firefighting experience alongside them.

Delta communities

Residents, boaters, and businesses across the Delta who benefit from boating safety education and from a safer, better-understood waterway.

The rivers themselves

The Sacramento, San Joaquin, and Mokelumne River ecosystems, through research, water quality testing, and levee integrity inspections.

On the Water

How the work gets done

  1. Agency request or joint exercise

    Work begins when an agency asks for assistance during a maritime emergency, or when members take part in training and exercises alongside public safety partners.

  2. The right discipline for the task

    Members are matched to what the situation needs: divers, boat crews and licensed captains, salvage and marine firefighting experience, or marine engineering.

  3. Training that carries into the next response

    Boating safety, rescue operations, marine engineering, and environmental conservation training keep members and partners ready between incidents.

  4. Stewardship between calls

    Research, water quality testing, and levee integrity inspections on the Sacramento, San Joaquin, and Mokelumne Rivers.
