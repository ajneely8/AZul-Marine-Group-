Who We Are

A community of marine professionals

Azul Marine Group brings together search and rescue personnel, divers, salvage specialists, marine firefighters, marine engineers, and licensed captains. We assist local, regional, state, and federal agencies during maritime emergencies, deliver marine training, and care for the Sacramento, San Joaquin, and Mokelumne Rivers.
