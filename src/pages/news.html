Azul Marine Group acquires retired Oakland fireboat Seawolf

A retired City of Oakland fireboat has a new mission. Azul Marine Group has acquired the fireboat Seawolf and is relocating her to J&H Marine in Stockton, where restoration will begin to return the vessel to operational status. Once restored, Seawolf will provide a platform for marine firefighting support, fire training and interagency exercises, search and rescue, water quality testing, and levee and shoreline inspection support on the Sacramento, San Joaquin, and Mokelumne Rivers. A public fundraising campaign is open to support relocation, shipyard work, inspections, and repairs.

Azul Marine Group introduces itself to the Delta community

The organization launched its public Facebook page with a statement of its purpose: to bring together marine professionals from search and rescue, diving, salvage, marine firefighting, marine engineering, and licensed captain backgrounds to support maritime emergencies and promote marine safety and education, in collaboration with local, regional, state, and federal agencies.
