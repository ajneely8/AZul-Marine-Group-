April 2026Public record

IRS recognizes Azul Marine Group as a 501(c)(3) public charity

The Internal Revenue Service lists The Azul Marine Group (EIN {{site.ein}}) as a tax-exempt organization under section 501(c)(3), classified as a public charity, with a ruling date of April 2026. Contributions to the organization are tax deductible to the extent allowed by law.

The organization is classified under NTEE code B90, Educational Services, reflecting the training and education portion of its mission.

March 16, 2025Announcement

Azul Marine Group introduces itself to the Delta community

The organization launched its public Facebook page with an introductory statement of its purpose: to bring together a community of marine professionals from search and rescue, diving, salvage, marine firefighting, marine engineering, and licensed captain backgrounds to support maritime emergencies and promote marine safety and education.

The announcement also set out the organization's commitment to collaborating with local, regional, state, and federal agencies, and its goal of protecting and enhancing the ecosystems of the Sacramento, San Joaquin, and Mokelumne Rivers through research, water quality testing, and levee integrity inspections.
