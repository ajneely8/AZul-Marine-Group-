March 16, 2025

Azul Marine Group introduces itself to the Delta community

The organization launched its public Facebook page with a statement of its purpose: to bring together marine professionals from search and rescue, diving, salvage, marine firefighting, marine engineering, and licensed captain backgrounds to support maritime emergencies and promote marine safety and education. The announcement also set out its commitment to collaborating with local, regional, state, and federal agencies and its goal of protecting the Sacramento, San Joaquin, and Mokelumne Rivers.
